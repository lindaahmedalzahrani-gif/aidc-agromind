Tomato - Viral Disease (Tomato Spotted Wilt Virus) / 番茄 - 病毒病

[Tomato | Viral Disease] Q: What causes this? A: Tomato spotted wilt is
caused by Tomato Spotted Wilt Virus (TSWV), a tospovirus transmitted
almost entirely by thrips, especially western flower thrips. Only
immature thrips can acquire the virus, but the adults they grow into
carry and spread it for the rest of their lives, so an early thrips
infestation is what actually introduces the disease.

[番茄 | 病毒病] 问：病因是什么？
答：番茄斑萎病由番茄斑萎病毒（TSWV）引起，主要通过蓟马传播，尤其是西花蓟马。只有蓟马幼虫期才能获得病毒，但它们长为成虫后会终生携带并传播病毒，因此早期的蓟马侵染是该病爆发的关键原因。

[Tomato | Viral Disease] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Look for
wilting combined with leaf curling and a purplish or bronze
discoloration on young leaves. As the disease progresses, ring-shaped
spots may appear on fruit, and plant growth becomes stunted and uneven.
These symptoms together (not any single one) are what distinguish TSWV
from ordinary wilting.

[番茄 | 病毒病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：注意萎蔫并伴随叶片卷曲，幼叶出现紫色或古铜色变色。病情发展后，果实上可能出现环状斑点，植株生长会变得矮小且不均匀。这些症状同时出现（而非单一症状）才是判断TSWV病毒病的关键依据。

[Tomato | Viral Disease] Q: How can I manage it? A: There is no cure
once a plant is infected, so management focuses on prevention: avoid
planting near onion, garlic, or cereal fields since thrips build up
heavily on them, use yellow sticky traps to monitor thrips populations,
and remove and destroy infected plants immediately (rogue them out) to
stop the virus from spreading to healthy plants nearby.

[番茄 | 病毒病] 问：如何防治？
答：植株一旦感染便无法治愈，因此防治重点在于预防：避免在洋葱、大蒜或谷类作物附近种植，因为蓟马容易在这些作物上大量繁殖；使用黄色粘虫板监测蓟马种群；发现感病植株应立即拔除销毁，以防病毒传播给周围健康植株。

[Tomato | Viral Disease] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Tomato is a
warm-season fruiting vegetable in the nightshade family, grown widely
for fresh consumption and processing. It is highly sensitive to
insect-vectored viruses, which is why thrips and aphid control is a
routine part of tomato pest management programs, separate from managing
fungal or bacterial diseases.

[番茄 | 病毒病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：番茄是茄科的一种喜温果菜，广泛用于鲜食和加工。番茄对昆虫传播的病毒病非常敏感，因此蓟马和蚜虫的防治是番茄病虫害管理中的常规工作，这与防治真菌或细菌性病害是分开进行的。

Tomato - Blight / 番茄 - 疫病

[Tomato | Blight] Q: What causes this? A: Blight in tomato (疫病) is
commonly caused by Phytophthora species, water-mold pathogens that
thrive in wet, poorly-drained soil and spread rapidly during periods of
rain or overhead irrigation. Infection usually starts at the stem base
or roots and moves upward through the plant.

[番茄 | 疫病] 问：病因是什么？
答：番茄疫病通常由疫霉菌（Phytophthora）引起，这是一种在潮湿、排水不良的土壤中容易滋生的病原菌，在多雨或喷灌频繁的条件下传播迅速。感染通常从茎基部或根部开始，逐渐向上蔓延。

[Tomato | Blight] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Early signs
include dark, water-soaked lesions on stems near the soil line and
sudden wilting of the whole plant even though the soil is moist. As the
disease advances, the stem base becomes soft and discolored, and the
plant can collapse within days under warm, wet conditions.

[番茄 | 疫病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：早期症状包括近地面茎部出现暗色水浸状病斑，即使土壤湿润植株也会突然萎蔫。病情加重后，茎基部变软并变色，在温暖潮湿条件下植株可能在几天内整株倒伏。

[Tomato | Blight] Q: How can I manage it? A: Improve drainage and avoid
overhead irrigation to reduce the wet conditions the pathogen needs,
rotate away from tomato and other nightshades for at least two to three
years in affected fields, and remove infected plants promptly since the
pathogen survives in soil and plant debris.

[番茄 | 疫病] 问：如何防治？
答：改善田间排水，避免喷灌，以减少病原菌所需的潮湿环境；受害地块应与番茄及其他茄科作物轮作至少两到三年；及时清除病株，因为病原菌可在土壤和植株残体中长期存活。

[Tomato | Blight] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Tomato performs
best in well-drained soil with consistent but not excessive moisture.
Because several of its most serious diseases, including blight, are
driven by wet conditions at the root zone, drainage and irrigation
management are as important to tomato health as pest control.

[番茄 | 疫病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：番茄在排水良好、水分适中但不过湿的土壤中长势最佳。由于包括疫病在内的多种严重病害都与根部积水有关，因此排水和灌溉管理对番茄的健康与虫害防治同样重要。

Tomato - Bacterial Disease / 番茄 - 细菌性

[Tomato | Bacterial Disease] Q: What causes this? A: Bacterial spot on
tomato is caused by Xanthomonas bacteria, which are seed-borne and can
also survive on infected transplants without showing symptoms until
conditions favor an outbreak. Warm temperatures above 20°C combined with
high humidity or splashing water (rain or overhead irrigation) are what
trigger visible disease.

[番茄 | 细菌性] 问：病因是什么？
答：番茄细菌性斑点病由黄单胞菌（Xanthomonas）引起，该病原可通过种子传播，也能在感病但未表现症状的种苗上潜伏，直到条件适宜时才爆发。20摄氏度以上的温度配合高湿度或雨水、喷灌溅水，是诱发明显病害的主要条件。

[Tomato | Bacterial Disease] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Look
for small, dark, water-soaked spots on leaves that turn brown to black
and feel slightly raised; leaf spot centers often dry out and tear,
giving leaves a ragged look. On fruit, spots start small and scabby and
can enlarge and merge as the disease progresses.

[番茄 | 细菌性] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：注意叶片上出现细小、暗色、水浸状斑点，随后变为褐色至黑色并略微隆起；病斑中心常常干裂，使叶片呈现破损状。果实上的病斑起初细小粗糙，病情加重后会扩大并连合。

[Tomato | Bacterial Disease] Q: How can I manage it? A: Use
pathogen-free seed and disease-free transplants whenever possible, since
this is the single most effective way to prevent an outbreak. Preventive
copper sprays combined with these cultural practices help manage the
disease once conditions become favorable, and avoiding overhead watering
reduces bacterial spread between plants.

[番茄 | 细菌性] 问：如何防治？
答：尽可能使用无病原种子和健康种苗，这是预防该病最有效的方法。在条件适宜发病时，配合预防性喷施铜制剂可帮助控制病情；避免喷灌浇水可减少细菌在植株间的传播。

[Tomato | Bacterial Disease] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Tomato
is grown in a wide range of climates but is particularly vulnerable to
bacterial diseases during warm, humid growing seasons. Because the
bacteria that cause spot diseases can hide symptomlessly in transplants,
sourcing clean seedlings is one of the most important tomato-growing
decisions a farmer makes.

[番茄 | 细菌性] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：番茄适应多种气候种植，但在温暖潮湿的生长季节中尤其容易感染细菌性病害。由于引起斑点病的细菌可在种苗上无症状潜伏，因此选用干净健康的种苗是种植番茄时最重要的决定之一。

Tomato - Blossom end rot / 番茄 - 脐腐病

[Tomato | Blossom end rot] Q: What causes this? A: Blossom end rot is a
physiological disorder, not an infectious disease. It develops when
rapidly growing fruit does not receive enough calcium, often because
irregular soil moisture, root stress, high salts, or overly rapid growth
disrupt calcium movement into the fruit.

[番茄 | 脐腐病] 问：病因是什么？
答：番茄脐腐病是一种生理性障碍，并非传染性病害。快速发育的果实得不到足够钙时会发生，常与土壤水分忽干忽湿、根系受压、盐分过高或生长过快影响钙运输有关。

[Tomato | Blossom end rot] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Look
for a pale or water-soaked spot at the blossom end of young fruit. It
enlarges and becomes brown to black, sunken, dry, and leathery;
secondary rots may later invade the damaged tissue.

[番茄 | 脐腐病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：幼果花端先出现浅色或水渍状斑点，随后扩大并变成褐色至黑色、凹陷、干燥和革质状，受损组织之后可能继发腐烂。

[Tomato | Blossom end rot] Q: How can I manage it? A: Keep soil moisture
even, mulch where appropriate, avoid root injury and excessive
fertilization, and use a soil test before adding calcium. Pesticides do
not cure blossom end rot, and damaged fruit will not recover.

[番茄 | 脐腐病] 问：如何防治？
答：保持土壤水分稳定，可适当覆盖地表；避免伤根和过量施肥。补钙前应先进行土壤检测。农药不能治愈脐腐病，已经受害的果实也不会恢复。

[Tomato | Blossom end rot] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Tomato is
a warm-season nightshade crop. Consistent irrigation, good air movement,
clean planting material, and regular scouting for insects and leaf
symptoms are central to reducing disease and pest pressure.

[番茄 | 脐腐病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：番茄是喜温的茄科作物。保持稳定灌溉、良好通风、使用健康种苗，并定期检查虫害和叶片症状，是降低病虫害风险的关键。

Tomato - Early Blight / 番茄 - 早疫病

[Tomato | Early Blight] Q: What causes this? A: Early blight is caused
by Alternaria solani, which survives on infected crop residue and
related weeds. Spores spread by wind and splashing water, and infection
is favored by prolonged leaf wetness and humid weather.

[番茄 | 早疫病] 问：病因是什么？
答：番茄早疫病主要由茄链格孢菌（Alternaria
solani）引起，病原可在病残体和茄科杂草上存活，孢子通过风和溅水传播，叶片长时间潮湿和高湿有利于发病。

[Tomato | Early Blight] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Older
leaves usually develop brown to black spots with concentric rings, often
described as target-like lesions. Severe infection causes yellowing and
leaf loss; fruit lesions are typically dark, dry, sunken, and may also
show rings.

[番茄 | 早疫病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：老叶通常先出现褐色至黑色病斑，常具有同心轮纹，呈靶纹状。严重时叶片黄化脱落，果实可出现暗色、干燥、凹陷并带轮纹的病斑。

[Tomato | Early Blight] Q: How can I manage it? A: Remove infected
debris and volunteer nightshades, rotate away from tomato and potato,
reduce prolonged leaf wetness, and use locally registered fungicides
only when disease pressure and local guidance justify treatment.

[番茄 | 早疫病] 问：如何防治？
答：清除病残体和自生茄科植株，与番茄和马铃薯等作物轮作，减少叶片长时间潮湿；在病害压力较高且当地建议需要时，再使用登记允许的杀菌剂。

[Tomato | Early Blight] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Tomato is a
warm-season nightshade crop. Consistent irrigation, good air movement,
clean planting material, and regular scouting for insects and leaf
symptoms are central to reducing disease and pest pressure.

[番茄 | 早疫病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：番茄是喜温的茄科作物。保持稳定灌溉、良好通风、使用健康种苗，并定期检查虫害和叶片症状，是降低病虫害风险的关键。

Tomato - Late Blight / 番茄 - 晚疫病

[Tomato | Late Blight] Q: What causes this? A: Late blight is caused by
Phytophthora infestans, an oomycete that spreads rapidly in cool, wet
conditions through airborne spores and infected plant material.

[番茄 | 晚疫病] 问：病因是什么？ 答：番茄晚疫病由疫霉菌 Phytophthora
infestans
引起，在凉爽潮湿条件下可通过空气传播的孢子和带病植株材料迅速扩散。

[Tomato | Late Blight] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Leaves
develop rapidly enlarging water-soaked or brown lesions, often with pale
or whitish sporulation near lesion margins in humid weather. Stems can
darken, and fruit may develop firm brown lesions.

[番茄 | 晚疫病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：叶片出现快速扩大的水渍状或褐色病斑，潮湿时病斑边缘可见浅色或白色霉状物；茎部可变暗，果实可出现坚实褐色病斑。

[Tomato | Late Blight] Q: How can I manage it? A: Use healthy
transplants, remove infected plants promptly, avoid leaving cull piles
or infected volunteer plants, improve airflow, and follow regional
late-blight alerts and registered fungicide guidance where available.

[番茄 | 晚疫病] 问：如何防治？
答：使用健康种苗，及时清除病株和病果堆，清除带病自生植株，改善通风，并根据当地晚疫病预警和登记用药指南进行防治。

[Tomato | Late Blight] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Tomato is a
warm-season nightshade crop. Consistent irrigation, good air movement,
clean planting material, and regular scouting for insects and leaf
symptoms are central to reducing disease and pest pressure.

[番茄 | 晚疫病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：番茄是喜温的茄科作物。保持稳定灌溉、良好通风、使用健康种苗，并定期检查虫害和叶片症状，是降低病虫害风险的关键。

Tomato - Powdery Mildew / 番茄 - 白粉病

[Tomato | Powdery Mildew] Q: What causes this? A: Tomato powdery mildew
is caused by powdery mildew fungi that spread by airborne spores.
Disease is often favored by moderate temperatures, dense canopies, and
conditions that support fungal growth even without free water on leaves.

[番茄 | 白粉病] 问：病因是什么？
答：番茄白粉病由白粉菌类真菌引起，孢子可随空气传播。适中温度、冠层过密等环境有利于病害发生，即使叶面没有明显积水也可发展。

[Tomato | Powdery Mildew] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Look
for white powdery patches on leaves that enlarge over time, with
associated yellowing, browning, and premature leaf drop when disease
becomes severe.

[番茄 | 白粉病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：叶片上出现白色粉状斑块并逐渐扩大，严重时伴随黄化、褐变和提前落叶。

[Tomato | Powdery Mildew] Q: How can I manage it? A: Improve plant
spacing and airflow, remove heavily affected tissue where practical,
avoid excessive nitrogen, and use resistant varieties or locally
registered fungicides when available and justified.

[番茄 | 白粉病] 问：如何防治？
答：改善株距和通风，适当清除严重受害组织，避免氮肥过量；条件允许时选用抗病品种，并按当地建议使用登记杀菌剂。

Tomato - Fusarium Wilt / 番茄 - 镰刀菌枯萎病

[Tomato | Fusarium Wilt] Q: What causes this? A: Fusarium wilt is caused
by soilborne Fusarium oxysporum strains that invade tomato roots and
vascular tissue. The pathogen can persist in soil and is favored when
susceptible plants are grown repeatedly in infested ground.

[番茄 | 镰刀菌枯萎病] 问：病因是什么？
答：番茄镰刀菌枯萎病由土传尖孢镰刀菌相关菌株引起，病原从根部侵入维管组织，可在土壤中长期存活，连续种植感病番茄会增加风险。

[Tomato | Fusarium Wilt] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Lower
leaves yellow and wilt first, often beginning on one side of the plant.
Wilting progresses upward, growth is stunted, and cutting the lower stem
may reveal brown vascular discoloration.

[番茄 | 镰刀菌枯萎病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：下部叶片先黄化和萎蔫，常先发生在植株一侧，随后向上发展，植株生长受抑；切开近地面茎部可见维管组织褐变。

[Tomato | Fusarium Wilt] Q: How can I manage it? A: Use resistant
cultivars and clean transplants, avoid moving contaminated soil, manage
root stress, and rotate with nonhost crops where useful. Severely
affected plants should be removed.

[番茄 | 镰刀菌枯萎病] 问：如何防治？
答：使用抗病品种和健康种苗，避免携带污染土壤，减少根系胁迫，并在条件适宜时与非寄主作物轮作。严重病株应及时清除。

[Tomato | Fusarium Wilt] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Tomato is a
warm-season nightshade crop. Consistent irrigation, good air movement,
clean planting material, and regular scouting for insects and leaf
symptoms are central to reducing disease and pest pressure.

Tomato - Whiteflies / 番茄 - 白粉虱

[Tomato | Whiteflies] Q: What causes this? A: Whitefly infestations
occur when small sap-feeding whiteflies colonize leaf undersides. Warm
conditions favor rapid population growth, and some whitefly species can
also transmit plant viruses.

[番茄 | 白粉虱] 问：病因是什么？
答：白粉虱侵染是由成虫和若虫在叶片背面刺吸汁液造成的。温暖条件下种群增长迅速，部分白粉虱种类还可传播植物病毒。

[Tomato | Whiteflies] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Look for
tiny white adults that fly when leaves are disturbed, scale-like
immature stages under leaves, sticky honeydew, sooty mold, yellowing,
and reduced vigor.

[番茄 | 白粉虱] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：轻触叶片时可见小型白色成虫飞起，叶背有鳞片状若虫，并可能出现蜜露、煤污、叶片黄化和植株长势下降。

[Tomato | Whiteflies] Q: How can I manage it? A: Monitor leaf undersides
and sticky traps, remove heavily infested plant material, control weeds
that host whiteflies, conserve natural enemies, and use selective
controls when thresholds justify treatment.

[番茄 | 白粉虱] 问：如何防治？
答：检查叶片背面并使用粘虫板监测，清除重度受害组织和寄主杂草，保护天敌，在虫口密度达到防治需要时优先采用选择性措施。

[Tomato | Whiteflies] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Tomato is a
warm-season nightshade crop. Consistent irrigation, good air movement,
clean planting material, and regular scouting for insects and leaf
symptoms are central to reducing disease and pest pressure.

[番茄 | 白粉虱] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：番茄是喜温的茄科作物。保持稳定灌溉、良好通风、使用健康种苗，并定期检查虫害和叶片症状，是降低病虫害风险的关键。

Pepper - Viral Disease / 辣椒 - 病毒病

[Pepper | Viral Disease] Q: What causes this? A: The main viral threat
to pepper is also Tomato Spotted Wilt Virus (TSWV), spread by thrips in
exactly the same way as in tomato. Peppers grown near onion, garlic,
cereal fields, or greenhouses growing cut flowers are at higher risk
because these host thrips populations that carry the virus.

[辣椒 | 病毒病] 问：病因是什么？
答：辣椒的主要病毒威胁同样是番茄斑萎病毒（TSWV），其传播方式与在番茄上完全相同，均由蓟马传播。种植在洋葱、大蒜、谷类作物附近或切花温室附近的辣椒风险更高，因为这些地方容易滋生携带病毒的蓟马种群。

[Pepper | Viral Disease] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Infected
plants show distorted new growth, leaf mottling, and ring or line
patterns on leaves and fruit. Growth is often stunted and uneven across
the field rather than affecting every plant equally, which is a useful
clue when distinguishing it from nutrient problems.

[辣椒 | 病毒病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：感病植株表现为新生组织畸形、叶片斑驳，叶片和果实上出现环状或条状斑纹。植株生长通常矮小且田间分布不均，并非所有植株均等受害，这是区分病毒病与营养问题的重要线索。

[Pepper | Viral Disease] Q: How can I manage it? A: Avoid planting
pepper adjacent to onion, garlic, or cereal crops that harbor thrips,
use reflective mulch early in the season to repel thrips before they
settle, and deploy yellow sticky traps for ongoing monitoring. Rogue out
and destroy infected plants as soon as they are found.

[辣椒 | 病毒病] 问：如何防治？
答：避免将辣椒种植在容易滋生蓟马的洋葱、大蒜或谷类作物旁边；生长季早期使用反光地膜驱避蓟马，防止其定殖；持续使用黄色粘虫板监测虫情。发现感病植株应立即拔除销毁。

[Pepper | Viral Disease] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Pepper is a
warm-season crop closely related to tomato and shares many of the same
insect and virus pressures. Its fruit set and quality are especially
sensitive to early-season stress, so controlling thrips before flowering
matters more for pepper than for many other vegetables.

[辣椒 | 病毒病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：辣椒是一种喜温作物，与番茄近缘，面临许多相同的虫害和病毒压力。辣椒的坐果和品质对生长早期的胁迫尤为敏感，因此在开花前控制蓟马对辣椒比对许多其他蔬菜更为重要。

Pepper - Aphids / 辣椒 - 蚜虫

[Pepper | Aphids] Q: What causes this? A: Aphid infestations occur when
green peach aphids and other species colonize the undersides of leaves
and new growth tips, reproducing rapidly in mild, dry weather. Aphids
are also a common way viruses get introduced into a pepper planting,
since they can pick up and transmit plant viruses as they feed.

[辣椒 | 蚜虫] 问：病因是什么？
答：蚜虫侵染是由于桃蚜等蚜虫种类在叶片背面和新梢上聚集繁殖，在温和干燥的天气条件下繁殖速度很快。蚜虫也是病毒传入辣椒地的常见途径，因为它们取食时可获取并传播植物病毒。

[Pepper | Aphids] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Look for
clusters of small, soft-bodied insects on the underside of leaves and
along stems, shiny sticky honeydew on leaves, shed white skins from
molting, curled or distorted new leaves, and the presence of ants
attracted to the honeydew.

[辣椒 | 蚜虫] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：留意叶片背面和茎秆上成簇的小型软体昆虫、叶片上发亮的粘性蜜露、蜕皮留下的白色皮壳、卷曲或畸形的新叶，以及被蜜露吸引而来的蚂蚁。

[Pepper | Aphids] Q: How can I manage it? A: Monitor weekly by
inspecting the underside of leaves and new growth directly, rather than
relying only on sticky traps, since aphid identification at the species
level matters for choosing the right biological control. Predatory lady
beetles and aphid parasitic wasps are effective biological controls
where infestations are moderate.

[辣椒 | 蚜虫] 问：如何防治？
答：每周直接检查叶片背面和新梢进行监测，不要仅依赖粘虫板，因为准确识别蚜虫种类对选择合适的生物防治方法很重要。在中等程度侵染下，瓢虫等捕食性天敌和蚜虫寄生蜂是有效的生物防治手段。

[Pepper | Aphids] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Pepper produces
new growth continuously through its fruiting season, and that tender new
growth is exactly what aphids prefer to colonize. This makes aphid
monitoring an ongoing task throughout the growing season rather than a
one-time inspection.

[辣椒 | 蚜虫] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：辣椒在整个结果期会持续长出新梢，而这些嫩梢正是蚜虫最喜欢聚集的部位。因此蚜虫监测需要贯穿整个生长季持续进行，而非一次性检查。

Pepper - Thrips / 辣椒 - 蓟马

[Pepper | Thrips] Q: What causes this? A: Thrips infest pepper directly
for feeding and, more seriously, act as the vector that introduces
Tomato Spotted Wilt Virus into the planting. The adults are the only
life stage that flies, though poorly, and are mostly spread by wind
currents and on clothing or plant material.

[辣椒 | 蓟马] 问：病因是什么？
答：蓟马直接取食辣椒造成危害，更严重的是它们是传播番茄斑萎病毒的媒介昆虫。成虫是唯一能飞行的虫态，但飞行能力较弱，主要通过风力以及附着在衣物或植株残体上传播扩散。

[Pepper | Thrips] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Feeding damage
distorts plant growth, deforms flowers, and leaves white-to-silvery
patches on emerging leaves that often contain tiny black fecal specks.
High populations cause visible silvering or scarring on leaf and fruit
surfaces along with edema-like blemishes on fruit.

[辣椒 | 蓟马] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：取食为害会导致植株生长畸形、花朵变形，新叶上出现白色至银白色斑块，斑块中常伴有黑色小粪便点。高密度虫害会在叶片和果实表面造成明显的银斑或疤痕，果实上还可能出现水肿状斑痕。

[Pepper | Thrips] Q: How can I manage it? A: Use yellow sticky traps to
monitor thrips movement (the same traps also catch aphids and
whiteflies), apply reflective mulch early in the season to repel
incoming thrips, and avoid planting near onion, garlic, or cereal fields
where thrips populations build up heavily.

[辣椒 | 蓟马] 问：如何防治？
答：使用黄色粘虫板监测蓟马活动情况（同一粘虫板也能诱集蚜虫和白粉虱）；生长季早期铺设反光地膜驱避迁入的蓟马；避免在容易大量滋生蓟马的洋葱、大蒜或谷类作物附近种植。

[Pepper | Thrips] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Pepper is one of
the key host crops for western flower thrips in field settings,
alongside tomato and lettuce. Because thrips damage and virus
transmission are closely linked in pepper, effective thrips management
is really virus-prevention management as well.

[辣椒 | 蓟马] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：辣椒与番茄、莴苣一样，是西花蓟马在田间的主要寄主作物之一。由于辣椒上蓟马的为害与病毒传播密切相关，有效防治蓟马实际上也就是在进行病毒病的预防。

Pepper - Mites / 辣椒 - 螨

[Pepper | Mites] Q: What causes this? A: Spider mite outbreaks on pepper
are favored by hot, dry weather, which speeds up mite reproduction, and
by the loss of natural predators, often from earlier broad-spectrum
insecticide use. Mites are found mainly on the undersides of leaves, out
of sight until damage is already visible from above.

[辣椒 | 螨] 问：病因是什么？
答：辣椒上红蜘蛛（叶螨）暴发的原因主要是炎热干燥的天气，这种气候会加速螨类繁殖；同时天敌的减少（通常由于此前使用了广谱杀虫剂）也是重要原因。螨类主要藏匿在叶片背面，往往等到叶面上已能看出受害症状时才被发现。

[Pepper | Mites] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Look for tiny
yellow or white stippling dots on the upper leaf surface, visible when
viewing leaves from above, while the mites themselves are found on the
underside. Heavy infestations produce fine webbing across leaves and can
cause overall leaf bronzing or drop.

[辣椒 | 螨] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：留意叶片正面出现的细小黄白色斑点（俗称”白斑”），从上方观察叶片即可看到，而螨虫本身则藏在叶片背面。严重侵染时叶片上会出现细密的蛛网，并可能导致叶片整体呈古铜色或脱落。

[Pepper | Mites] Q: How can I manage it? A: Treat as soon as mites are
found at any level, since populations multiply rapidly in hot, dry
conditions and become much harder to control once webbing covers the
plant. Miticides that are gentler on beneficial insects are preferred
where predatory mites are still present in the field.

[辣椒 | 螨] 问：如何防治？
答：一旦发现螨虫，无论密度高低都应立即处理，因为在炎热干燥条件下种群会迅速倍增，一旦蛛网覆盖全株就更难控制。在田间仍有捕食性螨类存在的情况下，优先选用对天敌较温和的杀螨剂。

[Pepper | Mites] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Pepper’s broad leaf
canopy and the tendency of mites to hide on leaf undersides mean visual
scouting from above the canopy alone will miss early infestations.
Turning leaves over during routine field checks is an important habit
for pepper growers.

[辣椒 | 螨] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：辣椒叶片冠层较大，而螨虫又习惯藏匿在叶背，因此仅从植株上方观察容易错过早期虫害。种植者在日常田间巡查时养成翻看叶片背面的习惯十分重要。

Pepper - Anthracnose / 辣椒 - 炭疽病

[Pepper | Anthracnose] Q: What causes this? A: Anthracnose on pepper is
caused by Colletotrichum fungal species that infect immature fruit while
it is still in the field, though visible symptoms often do not appear
until after harvest. No anthracnose-resistant pepper cultivar has yet
been developed, which is why this disease remains a persistent priority
in pepper production.

[辣椒 | 炭疽病] 问：病因是什么？
答：辣椒炭疽病由炭疽菌（Colletotrichum）真菌引起，该病原在果实尚未成熟时就已在田间侵染，但症状往往要到采后才明显表现出来。目前尚未培育出抗炭疽病的辣椒品种，因此该病始终是辣椒生产中需要重点关注的问题。

[Pepper | Anthracnose] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Early
lesions are small and water-soaked, expanding rapidly into sunken spots
that range from dark red to tan to black. As infection progresses, small
buff or salmon-colored spore masses appear scattered or in concentric
rings within the lesion.

[辣椒 | 炭疽病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：早期病斑细小、呈水浸状，会迅速扩大为凹陷斑，颜色从暗红色到黄褐色再到黑色不等。随着病情发展，病斑内会散生或呈同心圆状分布浅黄色至橙红色的孢子堆。

[Pepper | Anthracnose] Q: How can I manage it? A: Start with clean,
pathogen-free seed and rotate crops away from pepper and related hosts
to reduce carryover inoculum in the field. Preventive fungicide sprays
timed to periods of favorable warm, humid weather provide additional
protection where the disease has been a problem before.

[辣椒 | 炭疽病] 问：如何防治？
答：首先应使用无病原的洁净种子，并与辣椒及其近缘寄主作物轮作，以减少田间病原菌的残留积累。在此前曾发病的地块，可在温暖潮湿的天气条件到来前预防性喷施杀菌剂，以提供额外保护。

[Pepper | Anthracnose] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Because
anthracnose symptoms on pepper fruit frequently appear only after
harvest, post-harvest handling and storage conditions matter almost as
much as field management for controlling this particular disease.

[辣椒 | 炭疽病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：由于辣椒炭疽病的症状常常在采收后才显现，因此采后处理和贮藏条件对控制这种病害的重要性，几乎不亚于田间管理本身。

Pepper - Bacterial Spot / 辣椒 - 细菌性斑点病

[Pepper | Bacterial Spot] Q: What causes this? A: Pepper bacterial spot
is caused by Xanthomonas bacteria that can be carried on seed or
transplants. Warm, humid conditions and splashing water favor infection
and spread.

[辣椒 | 细菌性斑点病] 问：病因是什么？
答：辣椒细菌性斑点病由黄单胞菌引起，可通过种子或种苗传播。温暖高湿和溅水有利于侵染与扩散。

[Pepper | Bacterial Spot] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Leaves
develop small angular water-soaked spots that turn brown, stems may form
raised lesions, and fruit develops raised, rough, cracked brown spots.

[辣椒 | 细菌性斑点病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：叶片出现细小角形水渍状斑点并逐渐变褐，茎部可形成隆起病斑，果实出现隆起、粗糙、开裂的褐色斑点。

[Pepper | Bacterial Spot] Q: How can I manage it? A: Start with
pathogen-free seed or transplants, avoid overhead irrigation, rotate out
of pepper, remove crop residue, and follow local guidance for copper or
other registered bactericides where appropriate.

[辣椒 | 细菌性斑点病] 问：如何防治？
答：使用无病原种子或健康种苗，避免喷灌，与非寄主作物轮作并清除病残体；必要时根据当地建议使用登记的铜制剂或其他杀菌剂。

[Pepper | Bacterial Spot] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Pepper is
a warm-season nightshade crop closely related to tomato. It responds
best to steady moisture, healthy roots, good airflow, and early
monitoring for insects that can also transmit viruses.

[辣椒 | 细菌性斑点病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：辣椒是与番茄近缘的喜温茄科作物。稳定水分、健康根系、良好通风以及及早监测可能传播病毒的害虫，对保持辣椒健康十分重要。

Pepper - Powdery Mildew / 辣椒 - 白粉病

[Pepper | Powdery Mildew] Q: What causes this? A: Pepper powdery mildew

Pepper - Whiteflies / 辣椒 - 白粉虱

[Pepper | Whiteflies] Q: What causes this? A: Whiteflies reproduce on

Pepper - Blossom end rot / 辣椒 - 脐腐病

[Pepper | Blossom end rot] Q: What causes this? A: Pepper blossom end
rot is a physiological calcium-transport disorder usually associated
with uneven moisture, root stress, high salinity, or rapid fruit growth
rather than an infectious pathogen.

[辣椒 | 脐腐病] 问：病因是什么？
答：辣椒脐腐病是一种生理性钙运输障碍，通常与水分不稳定、根系受压、盐分过高或果实快速生长有关，并非由传染性病原引起。

[Pepper | Blossom end rot] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: A pale
or water-soaked area develops at the blossom end of fruit and becomes
dark, sunken, dry, and leathery as the fruit enlarges.

[辣椒 | 脐腐病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：果实花端出现浅色或水渍状斑块，随着果实膨大逐渐变暗、凹陷、干燥并呈革质状。

[Pepper | Blossom end rot] Q: How can I manage it? A: Maintain steady
soil moisture, avoid root injury and excessive fertilizer salts, mulch
where suitable, and test soil before adding calcium. Damaged fruit will
not recover.

[辣椒 | 脐腐病] 问：如何防治？
答：保持土壤水分稳定，避免伤根和肥盐过高，可适当覆盖地表；补钙前先检测土壤。已经受害的果实不会恢复。

[Pepper | Blossom end rot] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Pepper is
a warm-season nightshade crop closely related to tomato. It responds
best to steady moisture, healthy roots, good airflow, and early

Cucumber - Viral Disease / 黄瓜 - 病毒病

[Cucumber | Viral Disease] Q: What causes this? A: Cucumber mosaic virus
(CMV) has an unusually wide host range and is transmitted mainly by
aphids, which pick up the virus while feeding on infected plants or
weeds and carry it to healthy cucumber plants. It also spreads through
infected seed and mechanical contact during handling.

[黄瓜 | 病毒病] 问：病因是什么？
答：黄瓜花叶病毒（CMV）寄主范围异常广泛，主要通过蚜虫传播——蚜虫在感病植株或杂草上取食时获得病毒，再传染给健康的黄瓜植株。该病毒也可通过带毒种子及田间操作接触传播。

[Cucumber | Viral Disease] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A:
Symptoms vary by strain but commonly include a light-green to dark-green
mottled pattern on leaves, vein banding, interveinal yellowing, and leaf
distortion. In severe cases, dark green blisters can form, and symptoms
can superficially resemble herbicide injury, which sometimes leads to
misdiagnosis.

[黄瓜 | 病毒病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：症状因毒株不同而有差异，但常见表现为叶片呈浅绿至深绿相间的花叶斑驳、叶脉带状变色、脉间黄化及叶片畸形。严重时叶片会出现深绿色水泡状凸起，症状有时与除草剂药害相似，容易造成误诊。

[Cucumber | Viral Disease] Q: How can I manage it? A: Control the aphid
populations that spread the virus between plants, remove and destroy
infected plants promptly to reduce the source of new infections, and
control weeds around the field since many weed species serve as a
reservoir for the virus between cropping seasons.

[黄瓜 | 病毒病] 问：如何防治？
答：控制传播病毒的蚜虫种群；及时拔除并销毁感病植株，以减少新的传染源；同时清除田边杂草，因为许多杂草是该病毒在作物茬口之间的越冬寄主和储存库。

[Cucumber | Viral Disease] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Cucumber
belongs to the cucurbit family and, because CMV can also infect beans,
peppers, and many ornamental and weed species, managing this virus
effectively usually requires attention to what is growing in and around
the field, not just the cucumber crop itself.

[黄瓜 | 病毒病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：黄瓜属于葫芦科作物。由于黄瓜花叶病毒同样能感染豆类、辣椒以及多种观赏植物和杂草，因此要有效控制该病毒，通常需要关注田块内外的整体种植环境，而不仅仅是黄瓜本身。

Cucumber - Mites / 黄瓜 - 螨

[Cucumber | Mites] Q: What causes this? A: Spider mites on cucumber
build up during hot, dry conditions, particularly in high tunnels or
greenhouses where humidity is low. Populations can escalate quickly once
conditions favor them, especially if natural predators have been reduced
by earlier pesticide applications.

[黄瓜 | 螨] 问：病因是什么？
答：黄瓜上红蜘蛛（叶螨）多在炎热干燥的条件下大量发生，尤其是在湿度较低的大棚或温室中更为常见。一旦条件适宜，种群会迅速扩增，若此前的农药施用减少了天敌数量，情况会更加严重。

[Cucumber | Mites] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Early
infestation shows as light stippling on the upper leaf surface, with the
mites themselves visible on the underside if you look closely. As
populations grow, webbing can cover entire leaves and branches, and
heavily infested plants may show overall bronzing or curling of foliage.

[黄瓜 | 螨] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：早期侵染表现为叶片正面出现轻微的白色斑点，仔细查看叶片背面即可发现螨虫本身。随着种群增长，蛛网可能覆盖整片叶子和枝条，严重受害的植株叶片会出现整体古铜色或卷曲现象。

[Cucumber | Mites] Q: How can I manage it? A: Treat infestations as soon
as they are found at any level, since dry conditions inside tunnels or
greenhouses let mite populations multiply rapidly if left unchecked.
Materials that spare beneficial predatory mites and insects are
preferred when natural enemies are still present in the planting.

[黄瓜 | 螨] 问：如何防治？
答：一旦发现螨虫，无论密度高低都应及时处理，因为大棚或温室内的干燥环境会让螨虫种群在不加控制的情况下迅速倍增。若田间仍有天敌存在，应优先选用对捕食性螨类和益虫较温和的防治药剂。

[Cucumber | Mites] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Cucumber grown in
high tunnels or greenhouses faces a higher spider mite risk than
field-grown cucumber specifically because of the drier, more sheltered
growing environment, so growers using protected culture should scout for
mites more frequently.

[黄瓜 | 螨] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：与露地种植的黄瓜相比，大棚或温室栽培的黄瓜面临更高的红蜘蛛风险，这正是由于设施内更干燥、更封闭的生长环境所致，因此设施栽培的种植者应更频繁地巡查螨情。

Cucumber - Downy Mildew / 黄瓜 - 霜霉病

[Cucumber | Downy Mildew] Q: What causes this? A: Cucurbit downy mildew
is caused by a water-mold pathogen that spreads through the air over
long distances and establishes quickly once it reaches a field,
particularly on cucumber and cantaloupe, which are more susceptible than
other cucurbits to the strain that affects this crop group.

[黄瓜 | 霜霉病] 问：病因是什么？
答：黄瓜霜霉病由一种卵菌类病原引起，该病原可通过空气远距离传播，一旦到达田间便能迅速定殖，其中黄瓜和甜瓜对该病原所对应的致病型尤为易感，比其他葫芦科作物更容易发病。

[Cucumber | Downy Mildew] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Look
for angular, yellow spots on the upper leaf surface that are bounded by
leaf veins, giving them a blocky rather than round shape. On the
underside of the leaf, a grayish or purplish fuzzy growth may be visible
during humid conditions, especially in the early morning.

[黄瓜 | 霜霉病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：注意叶片正面出现受叶脉限制、呈方块状而非圆形的黄色角斑。在潮湿条件下，尤其是清晨，叶片背面可能可见灰色或紫灰色的霉状物。

[Cucumber | Downy Mildew] Q: How can I manage it? A: Choose
downy-mildew-resistant cucumber varieties where available, since
resistant varieties can extend the harvest window by several weeks
compared to susceptible ones. Apply protective fungicides preventively
once downy mildew has been detected or forecast in the surrounding
region, and scout regularly during humid periods.

[黄瓜 | 霜霉病] 问：如何防治？
答：尽量选用抗霜霉病的黄瓜品种，与感病品种相比，抗性品种可延长数周的采收期。一旦周边地区检测到或预测有霜霉病发生，应及时预防性喷施保护性杀菌剂，并在潮湿季节加强田间巡查。

[Cucumber | Downy Mildew] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Cucumber
is among the cucurbit crops most susceptible to downy mildew, and
because the pathogen spreads on wind currents across large distances,
regional disease alerts and forecasting systems are an important tool
for timing protective fungicide sprays correctly.

[黄瓜 | 霜霉病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：黄瓜是葫芦科作物中对霜霉病最为易感的品种之一，由于该病原可随气流远距离传播，区域性的病害预警和预测系统是把握保护性杀菌剂正确施用时机的重要工具。

Cucumber - Aphids / 黄瓜 - 蚜虫

[Cucumber | Aphids] Q: What causes this? A: Melon aphid and green peach
aphid are the main species affecting cucumber, colonizing the undersides
of leaves and new growth. Populations expand quickly in mild weather and
can be reintroduced repeatedly from nearby weeds or other host crops
throughout the season.

[黄瓜 | 蚜虫] 问：病因是什么？
答：为害黄瓜的主要蚜虫种类是瓜蚜和桃蚜，它们聚集在叶片背面和新梢上。在温和天气下种群扩增迅速，并可能在整个生长季节中反复从附近的杂草或其他寄主作物迁入。

[Cucumber | Aphids] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Look for
colonies of small soft-bodied insects under leaves, sticky honeydew
residue on foliage, sooty mold growing on the honeydew, curled or
distorted new leaves, and stunted growth on heavily infested plants.

[黄瓜 | 蚜虫] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：留意叶片背面成群的小型软体昆虫、叶面上粘性的蜜露残留物、蜜露上滋生的煤污病、卷曲或畸形的新叶，以及重度受害植株的生长迟缓。

[Cucumber | Aphids] Q: How can I manage it? A: Monitor the underside of
leaves weekly rather than relying only on sticky-trap counts, and
encourage or introduce natural predators such as lady beetles and aphid
parasites where infestation levels are still moderate. Controlling
nearby weed hosts reduces the number of aphids migrating into the
planting.

[黄瓜 | 蚜虫] 问：如何防治？
答：每周检查叶片背面进行监测，不要仅依靠粘虫板计数；在虫害程度尚属中等时，可引入或保护瓢虫、蚜虫寄生蜂等天敌。清除周边杂草寄主可减少迁入田间的蚜虫数量。

[Cucumber | Aphids] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Because aphids
are also the primary vector for cucumber mosaic virus, aphid management
on cucumber serves a dual purpose: limiting direct feeding damage and
reducing the risk of viral disease spreading through the planting.

[黄瓜 | 蚜虫] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：由于蚜虫同时也是黄瓜花叶病毒的主要传播媒介，因此黄瓜上的蚜虫防治具有双重意义：既能减少直接取食造成的损害，也能降低病毒病在田间传播的风险。

Cucumber - Anthracnose / 黄瓜 - 炭疽病

[Cucumber | Anthracnose] Q: What causes this? A: Cucumber anthracnose is
caused by Colletotrichum fungal species and, as with pepper, infection
can occur on fruit while it is still developing in the field even though
visible symptoms may not appear until later, including after harvest.

[黄瓜 | 炭疽病] 问：病因是什么？
答：黄瓜炭疽病由炭疽菌（Colletotrichum）引起，与辣椒炭疽病类似，感染可能在果实尚在田间发育时就已发生，但可见症状可能要到之后（包括采收后）才显现出来。

[Cucumber | Anthracnose] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Look for
water-soaked lesions that expand into sunken, dark spots on leaves and
fruit, sometimes developing concentric rings of spore masses within the
lesion as the infection matures.

[黄瓜 | 炭疽病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：留意叶片和果实上出现水浸状病斑，逐渐扩大为凹陷的暗色斑点，随着感染成熟，病斑内有时会形成同心圆状分布的孢子堆。

[Cucumber | Anthracnose] Q: How can I manage it? A: Use clean seed and
rotate cucumber away from other cucurbits to break the disease cycle in
the soil, and apply fungicide sprays preventively during warm, humid
periods when the disease is most likely to establish.

[黄瓜 | 炭疽病] 问：如何防治？
答：使用洁净种子，并与其他葫芦科作物轮作以打断土壤中的病害循环；在病害最容易发生的温暖潮湿季节，预防性喷施杀菌剂。

[Cucumber | Anthracnose] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Cucumber
shares several major fungal diseases, including anthracnose and downy
mildew, with other cucurbit crops such as melon and squash, so crop
rotation planning needs to account for the whole cucurbit family, not
cucumber alone.

[黄瓜 | 炭疽病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：黄瓜与甜瓜、南瓜等其他葫芦科作物共有多种主要真菌病害，包括炭疽病和霜霉病，因此在制定轮作计划时，需要考虑整个葫芦科作物群体，而不仅仅是黄瓜本身。

Cucumber - Powdery Mildew / 黄瓜 - 白粉病

[Cucumber | Powdery Mildew] Q: What causes this? A: Cucumber powdery

[Cucumber | Powdery Mildew] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Cucumber

Cucumber - Thrips / 黄瓜 - 蓟马

[Cucumber | Thrips] Q: What causes this? A: Thrips feed by rasping
cucumber leaves, flowers, and young fruit. Populations often increase
during warm, dry periods and can move in from surrounding vegetation.

[黄瓜 | 蓟马] 问：病因是什么？

causes silvery or pale scarring, tiny black fecal specks, distorted

Eggplant - Mites / 茄子 - 螨

[Eggplant | Mites] Q: What causes this? A: Spider mites infest eggplant
under the same hot, dry conditions that favor mites on other nightshade
crops like tomato and pepper. Reduced numbers of natural predator
insects, often following broad-spectrum insecticide use, also allow mite
populations to build up unchecked.

[茄子 | 螨] 问：病因是什么？
答：茄子上红蜘蛛的侵染与番茄、辣椒等其他茄科作物一样，是在炎热干燥的条件下发生的。此前使用广谱杀虫剂导致天敌昆虫数量减少，也会使螨类种群失去制约而大量繁殖。

[Eggplant | Mites] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Look for fine
stippling on the upper leaf surface and check the underside of leaves

leaves to bronze, curl, or drop prematurely.

[茄子 | 螨] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：留意叶片正面出现的细小白色斑点，并检查叶片背面是否有螨虫本身，因为不仔细观察很难发现它们。严重侵染时会产生明显的蛛网，并可能导致叶片古铜色、卷曲或提前脱落。

[Eggplant | Mites] Q: How can I manage it? A: Treat as soon as mites are
detected at any level, since hot conditions allow rapid population
growth once an infestation starts. Choose miticides that spare
beneficial predatory mites and insects when natural enemies are still
present in the field.

[茄子 | 螨] 问：如何防治？
答：一旦发现螨虫迹象，无论密度多少都应立即处理，因为在炎热条件下侵染一旦开始，种群会迅速增长。若田间仍有天敌存在，应选用对捕食性螨类和益虫较为温和的杀螨剂。

[Eggplant | Mites] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Eggplant is a
nightshade crop, closely related to tomato and pepper, and shares many

[茄子 | 螨] 问：介绍一下这种作物。

Corn - Chemical/Pesticide Damage / 玉米 - 药害

[Corn | Chemical Damage] Q: What causes this? A: Chemical damage
(phytotoxicity) in corn is most often caused by herbicide drift from a
nearby application, herbicide carryover from a previous season or crop,
tank contamination from residue left over in spray equipment, or
applying a chemical while the crop is under environmental stress.

[玉米 | 药害] 问：病因是什么？
答：玉米药害（植物毒害）最常见的原因包括附近喷药作业造成的飘移、上一季或上茬作物残留的除草剂、喷药设备清洗不彻底造成的药箱污染，以及在作物处于环境胁迫状态下施药。

[Corn | Chemical Damage] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Symptoms
depend on the herbicide’s mode of action: some cause malformed brace
roots, brittle stems, and stunting; others cause stunted growth with
yellowing in the youngest leaves and reddened veins; and some cause
leaves that fail to unroll properly, an injury pattern sometimes called
“buggy-whipping.”

[玉米 | 药害] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：症状因除草剂作用机制不同而异：有些会导致支持根畸形、茎秆变脆及植株矮化；有些会导致植株矮化并伴有最幼嫩叶片黄化、叶脉发红；还有些会导致叶片无法正常展开，形成俗称”鞭状卷叶”的受害症状。

[Corn | Chemical Damage] Q: How can I manage it? A: There is no way to
reverse chemical damage once it has occurred, so management is about
diagnosis and prevention: identify the likely herbicide by matching
symptoms to its mode of action, review recent spray records and nearby
field activity, and clean spray equipment thoroughly between different
chemical applications to prevent tank contamination.

[玉米 | 药害] 问：如何防治？
答：药害一旦发生便无法逆转，因此管理重点在于诊断和预防：根据症状对应除草剂的作用机制来判断可能的致害药剂，查阅近期施药记录及周边地块的作业情况，并在更换不同药剂时彻底清洗喷药设备以防止药箱污染。

[Corn | Chemical Damage] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Corn’s
tolerance to different herbicides varies significantly depending on the
specific product and its mode of action, so what is a safe, labeled
application for corn can still cause visible injury under certain
weather or soil conditions, or if used at the wrong growth stage.

[玉米 | 药害] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：玉米对不同除草剂的耐受性因产品及其作用机制的不同而有明显差异，因此即使是对玉米标注为安全的合规用药，在特定天气或土壤条件下，或在错误的生育阶段使用，仍可能造成明显药害。

[Corn | Northern Corn Leaf Blight] Q: What causes this? A: Northern corn

[玉米 | 大斑病] 问：病因是什么？ 答：玉米大斑病由 Exserohilum turcicum

[Corn | Northern Corn Leaf Blight] Q: What symptoms should I look for?

[玉米 | 大斑病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？

[Corn | Northern Corn Leaf Blight] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A:
Corn health depends on hybrid resistance, residue and rotation
management, balanced fertility, and regular scouting from vegetative
growth through grain fill because foliar diseases and insects can reduce
photosynthetic leaf area.

[玉米 | 大斑病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。

Chinese Cabbage - Downy Mildew / 白菜 - 霜霉病

[Chinese Cabbage | Downy Mildew] Q: What causes this? A: Downy mildew in
Chinese cabbage is caused by a water-mold pathogen (Peronospora) that is
favored by cool, moist conditions typical of temperate growing seasons,
though in tropical and subtropical regions it can occur year-round.
Infection can happen as early as the seedling stage.

[白菜 | 霜霉病] 问：病因是什么？
答：白菜霜霉病由一种卵菌类病原（霜霉属）引起，该病原在温带生长季常见的凉爽潮湿条件下最为活跃，但在热带和亚热带地区则可能全年发生。感染最早可从幼苗期就开始发生。

[Chinese Cabbage | Downy Mildew] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A:
Look for pale, angular spots on the upper leaf surface, often followed
by a grayish fuzzy growth on the underside during humid weather. Severe
infection commonly occurs later in the season during the heading stage
and can cause substantial yield loss if left unmanaged.

[白菜 | 霜霉病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：留意叶片正面出现的淡色角斑，潮湿天气下叶背常伴有灰色霉状物。严重感染通常发生在生长后期的结球阶段，若不加以防治可能造成显著减产。

[Chinese Cabbage | Downy Mildew] Q: How can I manage it? A: Apply
protective fungicides preventively before symptoms appear in high-risk
cool, wet periods, since the disease spreads fastest once established.
Note that plants weakened by downy mildew become more susceptible to
bacterial soft rot afterward, so managing downy mildew early also helps
prevent a secondary soft rot outbreak.

[白菜 | 霜霉病] 问：如何防治？
答：在凉爽潮湿的高风险时段，应在症状出现前预防性喷施杀菌剂，因为该病一旦发生便传播迅速。需要注意的是，感染霜霉病后植株会更容易继发软腐病，因此及早防治霜霉病也有助于预防后续的软腐病爆发。

[Chinese Cabbage | Downy Mildew] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A:
Chinese cabbage is a cool-season leafy vegetable, and downy mildew,
bacterial soft rot, and turnip mosaic virus together are considered the
three most serious diseases affecting it across Asia and other growing
regions.

[白菜 | 霜霉病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：白菜是一种喜凉的叶菜类蔬菜，霜霉病、细菌性软腐病和芜菁花叶病毒被认为是亚洲及其他种植地区危害白菜最严重的三大病害。

Chinese Cabbage - Bacterial Disease / 白菜 - 细菌性

[Chinese Cabbage | Bacterial Disease] Q: What causes this? A: Bacterial
soft rot in Chinese cabbage is caused by Pectobacterium bacteria, which
produce enzymes that break down the pectin holding plant cell walls
together. Plants that have already been weakened by downy mildew
infection are known to become more susceptible to soft rot afterward.

[白菜 | 细菌性] 问：病因是什么？
答：白菜细菌性软腐病由果胶杆菌（Pectobacterium）引起，该细菌能产生分解果胶质的酶，破坏维持植物细胞壁结构的果胶。已知感染过霜霉病而变弱的植株，之后更容易感染软腐病。

[Chinese Cabbage | Bacterial Disease] Q: What symptoms should I look
for? A: Infected tissue becomes rotted, watery, and slimy, often with a
distinctive unpleasant odor as the tissue breaks down. This is different
from fungal leaf spots, which stay dry and defined; soft rot tissue
collapses and turns mushy.

[白菜 | 细菌性] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：感病组织会变得腐烂、水渍化并呈粘滑状，组织分解时常伴有明显的异味。这与真菌性叶斑病不同，后者病斑保持干燥且边界清晰；而软腐病组织则会塌陷并变得糊状。

[Chinese Cabbage | Bacterial Disease] Q: How can I manage it? A: There
is no chemical cure once soft rot sets in, so prevention matters most:
manage downy mildew early since it predisposes plants to soft rot, avoid
mechanical wounding of plants during handling since bacteria enter
through wounds, and remove infected heads promptly to limit spread in
storage and transit.

[白菜 | 细菌性] 问：如何防治？
答：软腐病一旦发生便无化学治愈方法，因此预防最为关键：及早防治霜霉病，因为它会使植株更易感染软腐病；操作时避免造成机械损伤，因为细菌通常从伤口侵入；及时清除感病的白菜球，以限制其在贮藏和运输过程中的传播。

[Chinese Cabbage | Bacterial Disease] Q: Tell me more about this crop.
A: Chinese cabbage soft rot is a particular concern during transit and
storage after harvest, not just in the field, since the bacteria can
continue rotting harvested heads if conditions are warm and wet.

[白菜 | 细菌性] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：白菜软腐病不仅是田间生产中的问题，在采收后的运输和贮藏阶段同样值得高度关注，因为在温暖潮湿的条件下，细菌仍会继续腐蚀已采收的白菜球。

Chinese Cabbage - Black Rot / 白菜 - 黑腐病

[Chinese Cabbage | Black Rot] Q: What causes this? A: Black rot is
caused by Xanthomonas campestris pv. campestris, a bacterium that can
spread in contaminated seed, transplants, splashing water, and infected

[白菜 | 黑腐病] 问：病因是什么？ 答：白菜黑腐病由 Xanthomonas campestris

[Chinese Cabbage | Black Rot] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A:

[白菜 | 黑腐病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：叶缘出现向主脉延伸的黄色V形病斑，叶脉可变暗，严重时发生大面积黄化、萎蔫甚至结球腐烂。

[Chinese Cabbage | Black Rot] Q: How can I manage it? A: Use

sanitize tools and equipment.

[白菜 | 黑腐病] 问：如何防治？
答：使用无病种子和健康种苗，与非十字花科作物轮作，避免喷灌和植株潮湿时操作，清除病残体并清洁消毒工具设备。

[Chinese Cabbage | Black Rot] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A:
Chinese cabbage is a cool-season brassica. Clean transplants, good

develop rapidly.

[白菜 | 黑腐病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：白菜是喜凉的十字花科作物。使用健康种苗、保持良好排水、合理轮作并经常检查叶片和根系十分重要，因为细菌、真菌、病毒和鳞翅目幼虫问题都可能迅速发展。

Chinese Cabbage - Clubroot / 白菜 - 根肿病

[Chinese Cabbage | Clubroot] Q: What causes this? A: Clubroot is caused

[白菜 | 根肿病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？

[Chinese Cabbage | Clubroot] Q: How can I manage it? A: Avoid moving
contaminated soil, improve drainage, use resistant varieties where
available, rotate for long periods with non-brassica crops, and raise

[白菜 | 根肿病] 问：如何防治？
答：避免携带污染土壤，改善排水，尽量使用抗病品种，与非十字花科作物进行长期轮作，并根据土壤检测结果适当提高酸性土壤pH。

Citrus - Mites / 柑橘 - 螨

[Citrus | Mites] Q: What causes this? A: Citrus rust mite (also called
silver mite on lemons) and citrus broad mite are the main mite pests of
citrus, thriving in warm, humid conditions. These mites are microscopic,
requiring at least 10-20x magnification to see, so infestations are
usually noticed only after fruit damage appears.

[柑橘 | 螨] 问：病因是什么？
答：柑橘锈壁虱（在柠檬上也称银虱）和柑橘阔恙螨是柑橘上主要的螨类害虫，在温暖潮湿的条件下大量繁殖。这些螨虫极其微小，需要至少10到20倍放大镜才能看清，因此侵染通常要到果实出现受害症状后才被察觉。

[Citrus | Mites] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Rust mite
feeding turns the fruit surface silvery on lemons, rust-brown on ripe
oranges, or blackish on green oranges, an effect often called russeting
or bronzing. Broad mite damage instead causes young leaves to curl and
can leave scarred or cracked rind, mostly on the inner-canopy side of
young fruit.

[柑橘 | 螨] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：锈壁虱取食会使果皮表面呈现银色（柠檬）、锈褐色（成熟橙）或黑色（未成熟橙），这种现象常被称为”锈斑”或”古铜化”。阔恙螨的为害则表现为幼叶卷曲，并可能在幼果内膛一侧的果皮上留下疤痕或裂纹。

[Citrus | Mites] Q: How can I manage it? A: Monitor from early spring
through summer since this is when most damage occurs, checking young
foliage early in the season and shifting to fruit inspection as the
season progresses. Avoid unnecessary pesticide sprays, since they can
kill the predatory mites that naturally keep rust mite and broad mite
populations in check.

[柑橘 | 螨] 问：如何防治？
答：从早春到夏季持续监测，因为这是主要受害期，生长季早期应重点检查嫩叶，随季节推进转为检查果实。应避免不必要的农药喷施，因为这会杀死天然抑制锈壁虱和阔恙螨种群的捕食性螨类天敌。

[Citrus | Mites] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Citrus rust mite
damage affects only the rind’s appearance and does not harm the fruit’s

risk of disrupting biological mite control when deciding whether
treatment is worthwhile.

[柑橘 | 螨] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：柑橘锈壁虱造成的损害只影响果皮外观，并不损害果实的内在品质，因此种植者在决定是否需要施药防治时，往往需要在市场对外观的要求与破坏生物防治平衡的风险之间进行权衡。

[Cucumber | Cucumber Mosaic Virus] Q: What causes this? A: Cucumber
mosaic virus (CMV) is a viral disease of cucumber and many other hosts.
Plant viruses affecting cucurbits are commonly moved by insect vectors
such as aphids, and infected weeds or other host plants can act as virus

[Cucumber | Cucumber Mosaic Virus] Q: What symptoms should I look for?
A: Look for yellow-and-green mosaic or mottled patterns, distorted or

[Cucumber | Cucumber Mosaic Virus] Q: How can I manage it? A: Remove and
discard infected plants, control weeds that can serve as virus
reservoirs, reduce insect-vector pressure, and use resistant varieties

systemically infected, prevention and removal of infection sources are
central to management.

[Cucumber | Cucumber Mosaic Virus] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A:
Cucumber is a cucurbit crop that can be infected by several viruses,

[Corn | Southern Corn Rust] Q: What causes this? A: Southern corn rust
is a fungal rust disease of corn caused by Puccinia polysora. Rust
spores spread into corn-growing areas and can develop rapidly when
environmental conditions are favorable, making scouting and regional
disease reports important for timely management.

[Corn | Southern Corn Rust] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A:
Southern rust typically produces yellow-orange to orange pustules,
concentrated mainly on the upper leaf surface and often higher in the

[Corn | Southern Corn Rust] Q: How can I manage it? A: Scout corn

disease presence and pressure, weather, and current local extension
recommendations; always follow the product label.

[Corn | Southern Corn Rust] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Corn can

appearance, epidemiology, and potential impact. Correctly distinguishing
the two matters because southern rust can be more damaging and may
justify different management decisions depending on crop stage and
regional disease pressure.

Eggplant - Phytophthora Root and Crown Rot / 茄子 - 疫霉根腐和冠腐病

SOURCE: UC Statewide IPM Program — Phytophthora Root and Crown Rot on Peppers and Eggplant

SOURCE_URL: https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/phytophthora-root-and-crown-rot-on-peppers-and-eggplant/

[Eggplant | Phytophthora Root and Crown Rot] Q: What causes this? A:
Eggplant Phytophthora root and crown rot is caused by Phytophthora
capsici or P. parasitica. The pathogens survive in soil, and saturated
soil or contaminated irrigation water strongly favors infection and
spread.

[茄子 | 疫霉根腐和冠腐病] 问：病因是什么？ 答：茄子疫霉根腐和冠腐病由
Phytophthora capsici 或 P. parasitica
引起。病原可在土壤中存活，土壤饱和和受污染灌溉水会显著促进侵染和传播。

[Eggplant | Phytophthora Root and Crown Rot] Q: What symptoms should I
look for? A: Affected eggplants wilt rapidly and may die. Taproots and
lateral roots develop water-soaked dark-brown discoloration, lateral
roots are lost, and soil-line stem lesions can girdle the plant.

[茄子 | 疫霉根腐和冠腐病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：受害茄子会迅速萎蔫甚至死亡；主根和侧根出现水渍状深褐色变色，侧根减少，近土面的茎部病斑可环割植株。

[Eggplant | Phytophthora Root and Crown Rot] Q: How can I manage it? A:
Plant on raised beds in well-drained soil, manage drip irrigation
carefully, use clean seed and transplants, and use crop rotations that
exclude susceptible hosts where practical.

[茄子 | 疫霉根腐和冠腐病] 问：如何防治？
答：在排水良好的高畦种植，谨慎管理滴灌，使用洁净种子和健康种苗，并在可行时采用避开易感寄主的轮作。

[Eggplant | Phytophthora Root and Crown Rot] Q: Tell me more about this
crop. A: Eggplant is a nightshade crop related to pepper and tomato, and
the Phytophthora species causing this disease can also affect these
related crops.

[茄子 | 疫霉根腐和冠腐病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。
答：茄子属于茄科，与辣椒和番茄近缘；引起该病的疫霉种类也能危害这些近缘作物。

Eggplant - Powdery Mildew / 茄子 - 白粉病

SOURCE: UC Statewide IPM Program — Powdery Mildew on Vegetables

SOURCE_URL: https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/powdery-mildew-on-vegetables/

[Eggplant | Powdery Mildew] Q: What causes this? A: Eggplant powdery
mildew can be caused by Leveillula taurica. Powdery mildew spores spread
by wind, and moderate temperatures and shaded conditions generally favor
disease development without requiring free water on leaves.

[茄子 | 白粉病] 问：病因是什么？ 答：茄子白粉病可由 Leveillula taurica
引起。孢子随风传播，适中温度和较阴的环境通常有利于病害发展，而且侵染不需要叶面存在游离水。

[Eggplant | Powdery Mildew] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A:
Powdery mildew commonly produces white powdery spots or patches and may
cause yellowing, browning, leaf death, or leaf drop. Leveillula
infections can show yellow patches with relatively little visible
powdery growth.

[茄子 | 白粉病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：白粉病通常形成白色粉状斑点或斑块，并可导致叶片黄化、褐变、死亡或脱落；Leveillula
侵染有时主要表现为黄色斑块而可见白粉较少。

[Eggplant | Powdery Mildew] Q: How can I manage it? A: Use resistant
material when available, provide good air circulation and sunlight,
avoid excessive fertilizer, and follow locally appropriate fungicide
guidance if disease pressure warrants treatment.

[茄子 | 白粉病] 问：如何防治？
答：有条件时选用抗性材料，保持良好通风和光照，避免施肥过量；病害压力需要时按照当地建议使用适宜的杀菌剂。

[Eggplant | Powdery Mildew] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: UC IPM
specifically lists eggplant among vegetable hosts of powdery mildew and
identifies Leveillula taurica as a powdery mildew fungus affecting
eggplant.

[茄子 | 白粉病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。 答：UC IPM
明确将茄子列为白粉病寄主，并指出 Leveillula taurica 可侵染茄子。

Pepper - Verticillium Wilt / 辣椒 - 黄萎病

SOURCE: UC Statewide IPM Program — Verticillium Wilt on Peppers and Eggplant

SOURCE_URL: https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/verticillium-wilt-on-peppers-and-eggplant/

[Pepper | Verticillium Wilt] Q: What causes this? A: Pepper Verticillium
wilt is caused by the soilborne fungus Verticillium dahliae. Its
microsclerotia persist in soil and germinate near roots, with cool air
and soil temperatures favoring disease.

[辣椒 | 黄萎病] 问：病因是什么？ 答：辣椒黄萎病由土传真菌 Verticillium
dahliae
引起，其微菌核可在土壤中长期存活并在根部附近萌发，较凉的空气和土壤温度有利于发病。

[Pepper | Verticillium Wilt] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A:
Leaves wilt and their margins and tips become dry and brown. Light-brown
discoloration can develop in the vascular tissues of roots, stems, and
branches.

[辣椒 | 黄萎病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：叶片萎蔫，叶缘和叶尖逐渐干枯变褐；根、茎和枝条的维管组织可出现浅褐色变色。

[Pepper | Verticillium Wilt] Q: How can I manage it? A: Use sanitation
to avoid moving infested soil, avoid sites with a history of susceptible
hosts, rotate with nonhosts such as corn for about two years, and
consider soil solarization where practical.

[辣椒 | 黄萎病] 问：如何防治？
答：做好卫生管理避免携带污染土壤，避开曾种植易感寄主的地块，可与玉米等非寄主轮作约两年，并在可行时考虑土壤太阳能消毒。

[Pepper | Verticillium Wilt] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Pepper
and eggplant share susceptibility to Verticillium dahliae, a pathogen
with a very broad host range that can persist in soil for long periods.

[辣椒 | 黄萎病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。 答：辣椒和茄子都可感染
Verticillium dahliae；该病原寄主范围很广，并可在土壤中长期存活。

Squash - Downy Mildew / 南瓜类 - 霜霉病

SOURCE: UC Statewide IPM Program — Downy Mildew / Cucurbits

SOURCE_URL: https://ipm.ucanr.edu/agriculture/cucurbits/downy-mildew/

[Squash | Downy Mildew] Q: What causes this? A: Squash downy mildew is
caused by Pseudoperonospora cubensis. Spores spread by air currents,
rain splash, or sprinklers, and infection is favored by leaf wetness,
dew, cool weather, and overhead irrigation.

[南瓜类 | 霜霉病] 问：病因是什么？ 答：南瓜类霜霉病由 Pseudoperonospora
cubensis
引起。孢子可随气流、雨水飞溅或喷灌传播，叶面潮湿、露水、凉爽天气和喷灌有利于侵染。

[Squash | Downy Mildew] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Look for
pale green to yellow angular spots bounded by leaf veins. Spots can
merge and turn brown; in moist weather, white-to-purple growth may
appear on the lower leaf surface.

[南瓜类 | 霜霉病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：叶片出现受叶脉限制的淡绿至黄色角形病斑，病斑可融合并变褐；潮湿天气下叶背可出现白色至紫色霉层。

[Squash | Downy Mildew] Q: How can I manage it? A: Avoid overhead
irrigation, reduce canopy humidity, monitor from vegetative growth
through fruit development, and use resistant varieties where available.
Follow local treatment guidance when symptoms first appear.

[南瓜类 | 霜霉病] 问：如何防治？
答：避免喷灌，降低冠层湿度，从营养生长期到结果期持续监测；有条件时使用抗病品种，并在首次出现症状时依据当地指南采取防治措施。

[Squash | Downy Mildew] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Squash is a
cucurbit, and UC IPM states that downy mildew attacks all cucurbits,
although cucumber is the most commonly infected.

[南瓜类 | 霜霉病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。 答：南瓜类属于葫芦科。UC IPM
指出霜霉病可侵染所有葫芦科作物，其中黄瓜最常受害。

Pumpkin - Powdery Mildew / 南瓜 - 白粉病

SOURCE: UC Statewide IPM Program — Powdery Mildew on Cucurbits

SOURCE_URL: https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/powdery-mildew-on-cucurbits/

[Pumpkin | Powdery Mildew] Q: What causes this? A: Pumpkin powdery
mildew is caused by powdery mildew fungi of cucurbits, including
Podosphaera (Sphaerotheca) fuliginea and Golovinomyces (Erysiphe)
cichoracearum. Windborne spores spread the disease, which is favored by
moderate temperatures and shaded conditions.

[南瓜 | 白粉病] 问：病因是什么？ 答：南瓜白粉病由葫芦科白粉菌引起，包括
Podosphaera（Sphaerotheca）fuliginea 和
Golovinomyces（Erysiphe）cichoracearum。孢子随风传播，适中温度和较阴环境有利于发病。

[Pumpkin | Powdery Mildew] Q: What symptoms should I look for? A: Pale
yellow spots can develop first, followed by expanding white powdery
fungal growth on leaves, petioles, or stems. Severely affected leaves
become chlorotic, brown, papery, and may die.

[南瓜 | 白粉病] 问：应该注意哪些症状？
答：早期可出现淡黄色斑点，随后叶片、叶柄或茎上形成并扩大的白色粉状菌层；严重时叶片黄化、褐变、纸质化并死亡。

[Pumpkin | Powdery Mildew] Q: How can I manage it? A: Use resistant
varieties where available, maintain good sanitation and airflow, and
follow locally registered fungicide guidance when disease pressure
requires treatment. Avoid excessive canopy density.

[南瓜 | 白粉病] 问：如何防治？
答：有条件时选用抗病品种，保持田间卫生和良好通风；病害压力需要时按照当地登记指南使用杀菌剂，并避免冠层过密。

[Pumpkin | Powdery Mildew] Q: Tell me more about this crop. A: Pumpkin
is a cucurbit. UC IPM states that all cucurbits are susceptible to
powdery mildew, although disease frequency and severity vary among
cucurbit crops.

[南瓜 | 白粉病] 问：介绍一下这种作物。 答：南瓜属于葫芦科。UC IPM
指出所有葫芦科作物都可感染白粉病，但不同作物的发生频率和严重程度有所不同。
